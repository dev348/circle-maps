# Circle Maps

A small tool for stakeholder mapping in live conversations. It was built for round one of Nami's action research as a Bricoleur Fellow at ISDM.

Live at **https://maps.nami.org.in**

## What it does

- Two concentric-circle maps: one with the bricoleur at the centre, one with the student at the centre. Circle 1 is closest.
- An optional third map for a second role, such as CBCL faculty or programme manager.
- Session details at the top: who you are talking with, date, time and location.
- Optional energy tags on each name: + gives energy, − takes energy, ± both.
- A stakeholder list for reference, drawn from Nami's and Sakshi's maps.
- Save as PDF, Copy as text, Undo and Clear all.

## Privacy

Everything stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Press Clear all between conversations.
